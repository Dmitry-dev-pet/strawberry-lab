## Next sequence — complete fruit before repeated cosmetic sweeps

1. **Calyx + pedicel first**, then hairs if the structural gate passes. Reuse independent existing crown/attachment components where practical; no unrelated new engine. Build parameterized non-flat leaves (curvature, variation, thickness), stalk and root/seat relationship using current neutral stock in physical mm. Use the existing common shape map and coherent cutter policy, not a detached decoration. Preserve raw hull, original 264 seeds, rest mapping, fields, cavity and current material modes. Verify saved scene with autoexec disabled in whole, plane, bite, and deformed pose; report leaf/stalk attachment, actual cutter intersection policy, manifoldness where solids are intended, and unchanged baseline body/seed attributes. Native Cycles whole/front/three-quarter and at least one cut comparison. Mark any unimplemented live cut or deformation explicitly.
2. **Cavity / vessel coherence.** Compare actual longitudinal and oblique openings with reference and botanical evidence; improve wall variability and attached tissue, align lumen and vascular ring. Do not increase bridge count merely to imitate accessible code. Validate enclosed void orientation, absence of floating positive islands, preserved outer skin and seed contacts.
3. **Cell heterogeneity.** Use existing spatial fields and cap-local refinement, regional sizes/relief and suppression by vascular halo. Test both cross-sections and bite from one 3D model. No arbitrary global subdivision or uniform noise-only improvement.
4. **Coordinated colors/optics.** Use the existing common pigment/grade functions; isolate light rig from pigment and SSS, reduce orange/pale cast, preserve darker cavity wall and subtle vessel transitions. Compare whole, longitudinal, macro and bite under fixed render settings. No unbounded SSS/gloss/white-spoke sweeps.
5. **Torn bite and liquid.** Test coherent nonperiodic exposed tissue and cutter-shape effects separately; share actual cutter field with seeds and external organs. Do not repeat failed isolated droplet/film/face extrusion tuning. Verify geometry and matched visual comparisons before promoting.

No new varieties, web export, performance optimization campaign or expansion of infrastructure until full-fruit visual review. Record baseline, actual Blender outputs, QA and negative evidence with pinned hashes. Keep work in research PR(s) until explicitly accepted; no bulk merge of PR50 or prior visual-negative experiments. Existing main technical baseline remains unchanged by this document.
